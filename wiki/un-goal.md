# The UN target this challenge maps to

**Target 2.1:** "By 2030, end hunger and ensure access by all people, in particular the poor and people in vulnerable situations, including infants, to safe, nutritious and sufficient food all year round" — [UN SDG2 targets](../sources/un-sdg2-targets.md).

It's measured by two indicators: prevalence of undernourishment, and prevalence of moderate or severe food insecurity based on the Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES) — [UN SDG2 targets](../sources/un-sdg2-targets.md).

Goal 2 overall aims to **"End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition and promote sustainable agriculture"** — [UN SDG2 targets](../sources/un-sdg2-targets.md).

## Global scale, for context only

**"around 2.3 billion people were moderately or severely food insecure" in 2024**, and 8.2% of the global population were undernourished in 2024, down from 8.7% in 2022 — [UN SDG2 targets](../sources/un-sdg2-targets.md).

This is a global figure, measured a different way (FAO undernourishment / FIES, national and global populations) than Fresno County's own data in [Fresno County: the data](fresno-county.md). It's included here only to show the scale of the target this challenge sits under — it is not a number to compare against Fresno County's, and this wiki doesn't do that math.
