# Wiki index

Start here. Every claim in these pages traces back to a file in
[`sources/`](../sources/). This wiki is scoped to **Fresno County, California** —
our challenge: *"How might we help more families in Fresno County access
healthy, affordable food in their community?"*

| Page | Summary |
|---|---|
| [The UN target this challenge maps to](un-goal.md) | SDG Target 2.1 — the global goal this local work sits under — plus global scale figures kept clearly separate from Fresno's own numbers. |
| [Fresno County: the data](fresno-county.md) | Fresno County's food insecurity, child food insecurity, and Food Environment Index, alongside its adult obesity rate — and a flagged disagreement between two different "Fresno food hardship" figures. |
| [Programs already working on this in Fresno](programs-in-fresno.md) | Central California Food Bank (direct distribution, ~280,000 people/month) and Cultiva La Salud (changing the food environment itself) — two different approaches already active in the county. |
| [App concept: mapping obesity and food-access gaps](app-concept.md) | A proposed app overlaying obesity data and low-food-access areas to find Fresno County's highest-need neighborhoods, plus how to find and pitch companies, local government, and foundations to fund and sustain it. |
