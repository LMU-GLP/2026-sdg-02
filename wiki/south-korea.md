# South Korea: food insecurity among Seoul's elderly

## The headline number

**56.9% of 513 Seoul residents aged 65+ fell into the food-insecure group**, in an analysis of the Seoul Food Survey 2023 — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).

That 56.9% breaks down by a single-item question: 43.1% "enough and various kinds of food," 37.3% "enough but not always various kinds," 16.3% "sometimes not enough to eat," 3.3% "often not enough to eat" — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).

## Why, per the study

Reasons split by category: **environmental 38.0%, physical 37.5%, financial 24.5%** — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md). The single most common reason was "Insufficient money to purchase food" (24.5%), then "Insufficient time to purchase or prepare food" (21.6%), then **"Desired types of food are not available nearby" (14.9%)** — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).

So for Seoul's elderly, environment and physical ability to get and prepare food outweigh affordability alone, even though money is still the single largest individual reason.

## Who is most at risk, and what protects them

- **Elderly living alone were about four times more likely to be food insecure** (odds ratio 3.59, 95% CI 1.52–8.51) — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).
- Two factors were protective: higher **digital food literacy** (OR 0.63) and higher **food environment satisfaction** (OR 0.38) — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).
- Context: Korea's population aged 65+ passed nine million in 2024 (19.2% of the population), expected to pass 20% in 2025 — a "super-aged society" — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md).

## The study's own recommended fix

> "Educational programs designed to enhance the ability to purchase online food, use self-service kiosks in restaurants, and use food delivery apps would effectively decrease food insecurity among the elderly in Seoul." — [Park et al., 2025](../sources/korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md)

This is a recommendation the study makes from its own data, not a program that is already running — no source currently in this repo confirms such a program exists in Seoul.
