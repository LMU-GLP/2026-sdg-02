# Food Security in the U.S. — Key Statistics (USDA ERS)

- **Source:** USDA Economic Research Service, "Food Security in the U.S. — Key Statistics & Graphics"
- **URL:** https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/food-nutrition-assistance/food-security-in-the-u-s/key-statistics-graphics
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31 (page covers survey year 2024, last updated by USDA 2026-03-30)

## Key figures

- **"13.7 percent (18.3 million) of U.S. households were food insecure at some time during 2024."**
- Of those, split by severity: **"8.3 percent (11.1 million) of U.S. households had low food security in 2024"** and **"5.4 percent (7.2 million) of U.S. households had very low food security"** in 2024.
- **"86.3 percent (115.7 million) of U.S. households were food secure throughout 2024."**
- 2024's 13.7% is not a statistically significant change from 2023 (13.5%), but is significantly higher than the rate seen from 2016 through 2021.

## Direct quotes

> "13.7 percent (18.3 million) of U.S. households were food insecure at some time during 2024."

> "86.3 percent (115.7 million) of U.S. households were food secure throughout 2024."

## ⚠️ Do not average or directly equate this national 13.7% with LA County's 24%

This is a national U.S. figure; the LA County brief (see [la-food-insecurity.md](la-food-insecurity.md)) measures the same USDA module but for one county specifically, and LA's county rate (24%) is nearly double the national rate (13.7%). Both use the same USDA Household Food Security Module, so this comparison is valid and shows LA County running well above the national baseline — unlike the Seoul comparison, which is not valid (see [korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md](korea-elderly-food-insecurity.md)).

## Summary

Nationally, 13.7% of U.S. households (18.3 million) were food insecure at some point in 2024, with 5.4% (7.2 million) at the more severe "very low food security" level. The USDA uses the same measurement module as the LA County brief, so the two figures are directly comparable — and LA County's 24% rate is substantially higher than the national 13.7%.
