# Central California Food Bank — the food bank serving Fresno County

- **Source:** Bank of the Sierra community partner profile, cross-checked against
  Feeding America's affiliate page and a Wonderful Company CSR article about the
  same food bank.
- **URLs:**
  - https://community.bankofthesierra.com/central-california-food-bank/
  - https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank/central-california-food-bank
  - https://csr.wonderful.com/articles/ensuring-fresno-county-does-not-go-hungry
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- Service area: **"Fresno, Madera, Tulare, Kings, and Kern Counties of California."** Feeding America's own affiliate page calls it the food bank for that same multi-county region.
- **"provides food to more than 280,000 people each month, including nearly 100,000 children."**
- **"our donors and volunteers helped us provide access to over 33 million meals in Central California,"** with a stated goal that **"by 2025, our goal is to double in capacity to provide access to 66 million meals annually."**
- **"the Fresno region has the third worst food hardship rate in the nation at 22%."** No date is given for this figure on the page it appears on, and it is not the same figure as the 17% (2023) Fresno County food insecurity rate in [fresno-county-food-access-data.md](fresno-county-food-access-data.md) — see the note below.
- Programs named: **"Neighborhood Market and Mobile Pantry"** (fresh produce in low-income and rural areas), a **BackPack program** that **"supplies 1,300 elementary school children a backpack of food every Friday,"** a **School Pantry program**, a **CalFresh Outreach program**, the **USDA Emergency Food Assistance Program**, and a **Partner Pantry Program** working with **"more than 220 partner food pantries."**
- Older (2021-era) figures from the Wonderful Company CSR article, describing the same food bank at an earlier point: **"serves over 350,000 people each month"** and **"distributed 54 million pounds of food to neighbors in need in 2021,"** through **"300+ partner feeding sites."** The same article says a **$2.5 million donation from The Wonderful Company** funded **"The Wonderful Food Center"** (opened 2018), which **"triples the capacity of the food bank."**

## ⚠️ Two different "Fresno food insecurity" figures do not match

This source's own page states the Fresno region has a **22%** food hardship rate, with no date attached. [fresno-county-food-access-data.md](fresno-county-food-access-data.md) gives Fresno County's food insecurity rate as **17% (2023)**, from Feeding America's Map the Meal Gap by way of a different platform. Both are plausible — they may use different geographic boundaries ("Fresno region" vs. Fresno County) or a different data year — but nothing read so far reconciles them. Keep both, don't average them.

## ⚠️ People-served figures also don't reconcile across time

The 2021-era Wonderful Company article says the food bank served **"over 350,000 people each month"**; the more recently-read Bank of the Sierra profile says **"more than 280,000 people each month."** No source explains the drop. This may reflect a real decline, a change in how the count is done, or simply two snapshots years apart — it is not clear which, so don't present one as more current without saying so.

## Summary

Central California Food Bank is the primary emergency food distribution organization for Fresno County, running direct distribution (Neighborhood Market, Mobile Pantry, School Pantry, BackPack program) plus benefits navigation (CalFresh Outreach) across a five-county Central Valley region, and it has already attracted large-scale corporate funding from local agribusiness (The Wonderful Company's $2.5 million gift). Its own stated figures are internally inconsistent across the two years these sources were published, which is itself worth noting rather than smoothing over.
