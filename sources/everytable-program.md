# Everytable — income-scaled pricing for healthy meals (Los Angeles)

- **Source:** Wikipedia entry on Everytable, cross-checked against everytable.com
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everytable
- **Read on:** 2026-07-31

## Key figures

- Founded 2016 by Sam Polk and David Foster, headquartered in City of Industry, California (LA County). Operates as a public benefit corporation.
- **"As of 2026, operates more than 35 locations across California."**
- **Core model: "offers meals with pricing scaled to the income level of the neighborhoods it serves,"** using per-capita income data by zip code to set each location's prices, while designing each location to be individually profitable.
- During COVID-19, weekly production scaled from 30,000 to 180,000 meals, and the company sold **5.2 million meals total**, growing annual revenue from $6 million to $36 million over that period.
- Total funding raised since founding: **$87 million**, including $1 million from Rohan Oza via Shark Tank (2018).
- Meals start at approximately $7 (per everytable.com), with a stated goal of competing on price with fast food.

## Direct quotes

> "offers meals with pricing scaled to the income level of the neighborhoods it serves"

> the company aims to provide "affordable and healthy meals to the local community while remaining competitive against alternatives such as fast food restaurants"

## Summary

Everytable is an LA-founded public benefit corporation that sells fresh, scratch-cooked meals at prices scaled to each neighborhood's income level, rather than one fixed price — a direct, already-operating example of a business model built around the LA affordability problem this team's other sources describe. It has scaled to 35+ California locations and sold millions of meals, showing the model is viable at some scale, though the sources read so far don't say what share of LA's food-insecure population it actually reaches.
