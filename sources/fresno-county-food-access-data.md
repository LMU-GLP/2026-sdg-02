# Fresno County food access & health indicators (Healthy Fresno County Data)

- **Source:** Healthy Fresno County Data, a Conduent Healthy Communities Institute
  community health data platform used by Fresno County Public Health. Underlying
  data drawn from Feeding America's Map the Meal Gap (food insecurity indicators)
  and the California Health Interview Survey, or CHIS (obesity indicator).

## Key figures

- **Food Insecurity Rate: 17% (2023)** in Fresno County, versus 13.7% for California and 14.5% for the U.S. Fresno County is rated as "in the second worst 25% of U.S. Counties" on this indicator.
- **Child Food Insecurity Rate: 21.8% (2023)** in Fresno County, versus 16.9% for California and 18.4% for the U.S. "in the worst quarter among California counties."
- **Food Environment Index: 7.5** (scale 0 = worst, 10 = best), versus 8.7 for California and 7.6 for the U.S. Rated "in the worst 25% of CA Counties and the second worst 25% of U.S. counties." The index "combines two measures of food access: the percentage of the population that is low-income and has low access to a grocery store, and the percentage of the population that did not have access to a reliable source of food during the past year (food insecurity)," equally weighted.
- **Adults who are overweight or obese: 70.1% (2024, CHIS)** in Fresno County, versus 60.9% for California and 68.5% for the U.S. — "in the worst 25% of CA Counties."
- **Students eligible for the Free Lunch Program: 60.8%** (as of May 1, 2026).

## Summary

Fresno County scores worse than both California and the U.S. on every food-access indicator this platform tracks — food insecurity (17% vs. 13.7%/14.5%), child food insecurity (21.8% vs. 16.9%/18.4%), and the composite Food Environment Index (7.5 vs. 8.7/7.6) — while simultaneously showing an adult overweight-or-obesity rate (70.1%) well above both benchmarks (60.9%/68.5%). That combination — worse-than-average food access sitting alongside a higher-than-average overweight/obesity rate — is exactly the pairing this team's proposed app is built to map.
